About DJ Moreno Social Promoter

DJ Moreno Social Promoter is a desktop social media management application created to organize and manage promotional campaigns for music, DJ performances, releases, videos and live events.

The application provides a centralized interface for preparing promotional campaigns, scheduling content and connecting authorized social media accounts.

Features

Music Campaigns

Organize promotional campaigns for songs, releases, music videos and other music-related content.

DJ & Event Promotion

Manage promotional content for DJ appearances, nightclub events and scheduled performances.

Content Scheduling

Prepare and organize promotional posts according to campaign dates and selected social platforms.

Authorized Connections

Social media accounts are connected using the authorization mechanisms provided by each platform.

Social Media Integrations

DJ Moreno Social Promoter is designed to work with official social media APIs and authorization systems where available.

TikTok Integration

TikTok Login Kit is used to authorize a user's TikTok account. The TikTok Content Posting API may be used to upload or publish promotional videos selected by the authorized user.

The application does not publish content to a TikTok account without authorization from the account owner.

Privacy & Security

DJ Moreno Social Promoter is designed to use official authentication and authorization mechanisms supplied by connected social platforms.

The application does not require users to provide their social media passwords directly to DJ Moreno Social Promoter.

Contact

Questions regarding DJ Moreno Social Promoter, account authorization, privacy or application functionality may be submitted through the application's official contact channel.
