DJ Moreno Social Promoter

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DJ Moreno Social Promoter ("the App") handles information when users connect authorized social media accounts and use the App's promotional and content-publishing features.

1. Information We Access

DJ Moreno Social Promoter may access limited information from social media platforms when a user explicitly authorizes a connection through that platform's official authentication system.

Depending on the platform and permissions granted, this information may include basic account information such as account identifiers, display names, usernames and profile information necessary to identify the connected account.

2. TikTok Information

When a user connects TikTok, DJ Moreno Social Promoter uses TikTok's official authorization and API services.

The App may request the following TikTok permissions:

  • user.info.basic — used to identify and display basic information about the authorized TikTok account.
  • video.upload — used to upload video content selected by the user to TikTok.
  • video.publish — used to publish user-selected content to the authorized TikTok account when Direct Post functionality is enabled and permission has been granted.

DJ Moreno Social Promoter only uses these permissions to provide the functionality requested and authorized by the user.

3. Content Selected for Publishing

Users choose the promotional content they want to manage, upload or publish through the App.

This may include videos, captions, promotional text, music information, event information and related campaign content.

4. Authentication and Access Tokens

DJ Moreno Social Promoter does not require users to provide their TikTok or other social media passwords directly to the App.

Authentication is performed using authorization mechanisms supplied by the applicable social media platform.

When access tokens are required to maintain an authorized connection, the App is designed to store them securely and use them only for authorized API requests.

5. How Information Is Used

Information accessed through connected social media accounts is used to provide App functionality, including:

  • Identifying authorized social media accounts.
  • Managing promotional campaigns.
  • Preparing and scheduling promotional content.
  • Uploading user-selected content.
  • Publishing user-selected content when authorized by the user.

6. Sharing of Information

DJ Moreno Social Promoter does not sell personal information.

Information may be transmitted to a connected social media platform when necessary to perform an action requested by the user, such as uploading or publishing content.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or a valid legal process.

7. Data Retention

Information is retained only as reasonably necessary to provide the App's functionality, maintain authorized account connections, satisfy legal obligations or protect the security and integrity of the App.

8. Revoking Social Media Access

Users may revoke a social media connection through the applicable social media platform's account or authorization settings.

When available, users may also disconnect an account from within DJ Moreno Social Promoter.

9. Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of personal information associated with DJ Moreno Social Promoter through the contact method provided on the official website.

Users may also revoke authorization directly through the connected social media platform.

10. Third-Party Platforms

Connected social media platforms operate independently and maintain their own privacy policies, terms and data practices.

Use of TikTok and other connected services remains subject to the privacy policies and terms of those respective platforms.

11. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used to protect information handled by DJ Moreno Social Promoter.

However, no electronic storage or internet transmission system can guarantee absolute security.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the App, its integrations or applicable privacy requirements change.

The effective date shown at the top of this page will indicate the latest revision.

13. Contact

Questions, privacy inquiries or data deletion requests regarding DJ Moreno Social Promoter may be submitted through the contact information provided on the application's official website.
