PostRitmo

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PostRitmo ("the App") handles information when users create an account, connect authorized social media accounts, and use the App's social media management, scheduling, uploading and publishing features.

1. PostRitmo Account Information

Certain features of PostRitmo may require users to create or sign in to a PostRitmo account.

Account authentication may be provided using Firebase Authentication. Information necessary to authenticate and identify a PostRitmo account may be processed by the authentication service.

PostRitmo uses account authentication to associate authorized connections and App functionality with the appropriate user.

2. Information From Connected Social Media Accounts

PostRitmo may access limited information from social media platforms when a user explicitly authorizes a connection through that platform's official authentication and authorization system.

Depending on the platform and permissions granted, this information may include account identifiers, display names, usernames, profile information and other information necessary to identify and operate the authorized connection.

3. TikTok Information

When a user connects TikTok, PostRitmo uses TikTok's official authorization and API services.

PostRitmo may request the following TikTok permissions:

  • user.info.basic — used to identify and display basic information about the authorized TikTok account.
  • video.upload — used to upload video content selected by the user to TikTok, including content submitted as a draft when applicable.
  • video.publish — used to publish user-selected content directly to the authorized TikTok account when Direct Post functionality is enabled and the user has granted the required permission.

PostRitmo uses these permissions only to provide functionality requested and authorized by the user.

4. Content Selected for Publishing

Users choose the content they want to prepare, schedule, upload or publish through PostRitmo.

This may include videos, images, captions, promotional text, music information, event information, release information and related campaign content.

Content is transmitted to a connected platform only as necessary to perform functionality requested by the user.

5. Authentication and Access Tokens

PostRitmo does not require users to provide their TikTok or other social media passwords directly to the App.

Social media authentication is performed using authorization mechanisms supplied by the applicable platform.

When access or refresh tokens are required to maintain an authorized connection, they may be securely retained and associated with the applicable PostRitmo account for the purpose of performing authorized API requests.

These credentials are not used to access social media accounts beyond the permissions granted by the user.

6. How Information Is Used

Information handled by PostRitmo may be used to:

  • Authenticate PostRitmo users.
  • Identify authorized social media accounts.
  • Maintain authorized social media connections.
  • Manage social media and promotional campaigns.
  • Prepare and schedule content.
  • Upload user-selected content.
  • Publish user-selected content when authorized.
  • Provide account connection and disconnection functionality.
  • Maintain the security and operation of the App.

7. Sharing of Information

PostRitmo does not sell personal information.

Information may be transmitted to a connected social media platform when necessary to perform an action requested by the user, such as connecting an account, uploading content or publishing content.

Information may also be processed by service providers used to operate PostRitmo, including authentication and hosting infrastructure, where necessary to provide App functionality.

Information may be disclosed when required by applicable law or valid legal process.

8. Data Retention

Information is retained only as reasonably necessary to provide PostRitmo functionality, maintain authorized account connections, satisfy applicable legal obligations, resolve disputes, or protect the security and integrity of the App.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the purpose for which it is processed.

9. Revoking Social Media Access

Users may revoke a social media connection through the applicable social media platform's authorization or account settings.

Where supported, users may also disconnect an authorized account from within PostRitmo.

10. Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of personal information associated with their PostRitmo account through the contact method provided on the official website.

Users may also revoke social media authorization directly through the applicable connected platform.

Certain information may be retained when required by law or where reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution or enforcement of applicable agreements.

11. Third-Party Services and Platforms

PostRitmo may use third-party services and infrastructure, including authentication, hosting and social media platform APIs.

Connected social media platforms operate independently and maintain their own privacy policies, terms and data practices.

Use of TikTok and other connected services remains subject to the privacy policies and terms of those respective platforms.

12. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used to protect information handled by PostRitmo.

However, no electronic storage, authentication or internet transmission system can guarantee absolute security.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as PostRitmo, its integrations or applicable privacy requirements change.

The effective date shown at the top of this page identifies the latest revision.

14. Contact

Questions, privacy inquiries or data deletion requests regarding PostRitmo may be submitted through the contact information provided on the application's official website.
